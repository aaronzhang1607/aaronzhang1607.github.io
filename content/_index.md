## Research Interest

<div class="grid-list">
<div class="g-num">[1]</div>
<div class="g-body">
<p><strong>Single-cell multi-omics with computational simulation.</strong></p>
<p>By using the CRISPRi system and single-cell sequencing, distinct gene expression patterns can be detected at the same time. With the help of machine-learning algorithms, a phenotype prediction model of gene knock-outs may change the way research is started.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="grid-list">
<div class="g-num">[2]</div>
<div class="g-body">
<p><strong>Fundamental physiology regulation mechanisms.</strong></p>
<p>Basic behaviors are usually the most important and essential — once living beings lose control of them, survival is at stake. Understanding their regulation will help us better resolve the magic biological world.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="grid-list">
<div class="g-num">[3]</div>
<div class="g-body">
<p><strong>Universal single-cell RNA modification sequencing techniques.</strong></p>
<p>Tagmentation of RNA/DNA hybrids still keeps the RNA strand, which may preserve the RNA modification information. This may help stabilize the RNA and make unbiased single-cell-level RNA modification research possible.</p>
</div>
</div>

## Research Experience

<div class="grid-list">
<div class="g-num">[1]</div>
<div class="g-body">
<h3 class="g-title">Thermoregulation under Hibernation-like State in Rodents with Single-Cell Sequencing</h3>
<p class="experience-meta"><em>Aug 2020 – Present · Adviser: Dr. Tongfei Wang · CIBR</em></p>
<ul>
<li>Studying the hypothermia state by combining single-cell RNA-seq with behavioral analysis to understand temperature regulation mechanisms, using ground squirrel, mouse, and hamster.</li>
<li>With single-cell RNA sequencing, ground squirrel's POA and DMH cells show state-difference patterns. Further analysis with a high-quality reference genome for ground squirrel should find de novo markers for temperature regulation.</li>
<li>Induced daily torpor in mice with fasting and collected cell samples with SMART-Seq3xpress.</li>
<li>Developing a new multi-omics sequencing method aimed at optimizing the time schedule and improving library quality.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>

<div class="grid-list">
<div class="g-num">[2]</div>
<div class="g-body">
<h3 class="g-title">YTHDF2 Function in <em>Xenopus tropicalis</em>' Early Development</h3>
<p class="experience-meta"><em>Bachelor Thesis · Jun 2019 – Jun 2020 · Adviser: Assoc. Prof. Yonglong Chen · SUSTech</em></p>
<ul>
<li>CRISPR/Cas9 knock-out of <em>ythdf2</em> in <em>Xenopus tropicalis</em> with phenotypic and genetic analysis.</li>
<li>First-generation <em>ythdf2</em><sup>-/-</sup> animals show no difference from normal in overall developmental appearance.</li>
<li><em>ythdf2</em><sup>-/-</sup> embryos developed slower than usual and failed to pass stage 8, while <em>ythdf2</em><sup>-/-</sup> sperm did not influence development.</li>
<li>YTHDF2 protein shows maternal-dependent effects in embryos, either by direct influence or by affecting other proteins' translation process.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>

<div class="grid-list">
